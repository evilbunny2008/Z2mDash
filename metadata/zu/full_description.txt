I-Z2M Dash iyideshibhodi ye-Android yamahhala nevulekile ye-Zigbee2MQTT. Ixhumana ngqo nebhulokha yakho ye-MQTT, ngakho akudingeki i-akhawunti yefu futhi awuboshelwa kunoma yimuphi umhlinzeki. Konke ukusetha kwakho kungalondolozwa efayeleni elilodwa, noma kwabelwane ngakho phakathi kwamafoni ngebhulokha uqobo.

Ideshibhodi
• Amaqembu amaqoqo, iqoqo ngalinye liqukethe amathayela edivayisi eyodwa: ukufundwa kwezinzwa, amaswishi okuvula/ukuvala nezinkinobho zokucindezela kanye (njenge-STOP yekhetheni).
• Amathayela ezinzwa anamayunithi nezindawo zedesimali ozikhethelayo, kanye nobubanzi obufanele obungakhethwa obufaka umbala ethayeleni uma ukufundwa kuphansi kakhulu noma kuphezulu kakhulu.
• Amaswishi abonisa isimo sangempela sedivayisi, hhayi nje umyalo wokugcina othunyelwe.
• Izinzwa zokuba khona nezokuhlala zivela njengo-"Kutholakele" noma "Akukho muntu".
• Hudula bese wehlisa ukuze uhlele kabusha amaqembu, amaqoqo namathayela, ngokuhlehlisa. "Khiya Isakhiwo" (kuvuliwe ngokuzenzakalelayo) kuvimbela ukuhambisa ngephutha.
• Amaqoqo athola uhlaka uma idivayisi ingabikanga isikhathi esingaphezu kwehora (obomvu), inebhethri eliphansi (oluhlaza okwesibhakabhaka) noma isignali ye-Zigbee ebuthakathaka (ophuzi). Amaqoqo asebenzisa amabhethri e-NiMH ashajwayo abalwa njengaphansi kuphela ku-5%.
• Imenyu yezihlungi ibonisa kuphela amadivayisi anebhethri eliphansi noma isignali ebuthakathaka, noma izinzwa zomswakama ezingaphansi noma ngaphezu kobubanzi bazo obufanele.
• Isebenza kumafoni naku-Android TV.

Ukusetha nokuvumelanisa
• Amadivayisi angachaza amathayela awo emlayezweni wokulungiselela we-"/app" ogciniwe (retained), ngakho wonke amafoni akubhulokha efanayo abonisa isakhiwo esifanayo, futhi izinguquko zefoni eyodwa zifinyelela kwamanye.
• "Thola izinzwa" iskena ibhulokha ifuna ukufundwa bese yakha amathayela amadivayisi angenako ukulungiselela kwe-"/app".
• Ibha ethi "Vumela ukujoyina" ivumela amadivayisi amasha e-Zigbee ukuthi ajoyine nganoma iyiphi i-router, ngokubala emuva.
• Amabhulokha amaningi nge-TCP, SSL, WS noma WSS, kuhlanganise nezitifiketi ezisayinwe ngokwazo.
• Uxhumano lwangemuva olungakhethwa lugcina ukufundwa nezexwayiso kusesikhathini ngisho noma uhlelo lokusebenza luvaliwe.

Izexwayiso
• Izexwayiso zentuthu ezivela kunoma iyiphi idivayisi ebika intuthu, ngomsindo we-alamu omkhulu ongakhethwa.
• Izexwayiso zokunisela, ngensimbi nokudlidliza, uma inzwa yomswakama ikhuphuka idlula maphakathi nobubanzi bayo obufanele.
• Izexwayiso zebhethri eliphansi ezibonisa iphesenti lebhethri.

Ukulondoloza nokulungisa amaphutha
• Thumela ngaphandle konke ukulungiselela, noma amabhulokha kuphela, njengefayela elicindezelwe. Okulondoloziwe kubethelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo (AES-256-GCM).
• Buyisela konke, noma uhlanganise amabhulokha kuphela.
• Noma shicilela futhi ubuyisele okulondoloziwe ngomlayezo we-MQTT ogciniwe, ngaphandle kokuhambisa ifayela.
• "Itheminali" ebukhoma engahlungwa ibonisa wonke umlayezo we-MQTT, ukuze uhlole okuqukethwe nezihloko.
