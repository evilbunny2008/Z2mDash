- Any change that gives a tile or cluster a new home - duplicating a cluster, adding or re-clustering a panel, dragging a tile out to its own cluster, or dragging a cluster to a new position or group - now publishes a retained "/app" config for it if one doesn't already exist, so it's a real, shareable device config (visible to other phones on the broker, and editable the same way as any other auto-configured cluster) instead of only existing in this phone's local config.
