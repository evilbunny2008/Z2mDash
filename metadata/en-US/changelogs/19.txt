- Added a "Blink Warnings" setting for out-of-range sensor tile alerts (moisture, humidity, etc.) - pulse (default) or stay a static color, your choice. The "last seen" stale-data indicator stays a flat color regardless, since blinking there wasn't noticeable enough to be worth it
- Fixed a race condition where dragging to reorder clusters or panels within a group could revert after a drag or two, especially with the app running on more than one phone against the same broker
- Reordering clusters/panels now properly syncs across every phone connected to the same broker, instead of only applying on the phone you dragged on
- Added "Import via code" on the Add Broker screen: shows a code/QR that lets you securely receive a saved broker's settings from mx3launcher.odiousapps.com, instead of typing them by hand - only the hostname is required, everything else (protocol, port, auth, base topic, and more) is optional and defaults to the app's own normal defaults
- Added an "Auto-Accept Discovered Devices" setting per broker, to skip the accept/ignore prompt for newly-seen devices and add their group/clusters/panels immediately
- Fixed deleting a broker leaving its panels and tracked devices behind - re-adding that broker afterwards (by hand or via import) could double up every cluster instead of just reconnecting to the same one
- Added a "Clean Up Orphaned Data" option in Settings (only shown when there's something to clean) to remove any panels left over from a broker deleted before the above fix
- Fixed tapping "Done" on the very first broker (added from the "no brokers configured" welcome screen) landing back on that welcome screen instead of Home, needing an extra tap back to actually see your dashboard
- Added a "Tile & Cluster Width" slider in Settings, so tiles/clusters can be widened to fill the screen instead of stopping at a fixed 110dp
- Added proper Android TV support: the app now appears on the TV's own launcher, the remote's D-pad actually works throughout the app (previously nothing ever had focus, so button presses did nothing at all), text fields no longer trap focus (Back now reliably backs out of one in a single press), tiles show a visible focus ring when navigated to with a remote, and TV uses a side navigation rail instead of the phone's bottom bar
- Fixed a sensor tile's value getting cut off with "…" on a narrow tile (e.g. "30.0°C" showing as "30…", losing the actual number) - the unit is now just clipped instead, so the number itself always stays fully visible
- Fixed the 3rd (rightmost) column of a cluster card being narrower than the other 2 and clipping its tile's content - the card wasn't sized wide enough to also fit its own internal padding around the row of tiles

- Fixed "Background Work" (off) not actually stopping broker connections when the app is backgrounded/closed - they previously kept running for as long as the process happened to survive regardless of the setting, since nothing ever disconnected them. Also added a check on returning to the app that reconnects and refreshes subscriptions if a connection quietly died while backgrounded, even with Background Work on - previously nothing detected that, so tiles could keep showing payloads from days ago with no way to tell they were stale versus just quiet
- Fixed the home screen recomposing (and redrawing) every single tile on the entire dashboard on every incoming MQTT message, even for topics unrelated to what that tile shows - each tile now only updates when its own specific value actually changes, which should noticeably cut down on dropped frames on screens with a lot of tiles/devices
- Fixed TV: opening a panel from a scrolled-down position on Home and pressing Back landed back on Home scrolled to the very top instead of where you were - the nav rail's structure was subtly different on the 3 main screens vs. every other screen, which was quietly resetting Home's state on the way back
- Added a safety net so a cluster's device name can never render as a tall, narrow vertical strip of single characters (something users reported on TV, cause not yet fully tracked down) - it now truncates with "…" if its width is ever unexpectedly too narrow, instead of wrapping into that vertical artifact
- Added a "Permit Join" toggle on each broker's edit screen, to open or close that Zigbee network to new devices joining without needing a separate tool - optionally scoped to a specific router (or the coordinator) by name, or left blank to permit joining via the whole network at once. Reflects the actual current state reported back by Zigbee2MQTT, not just what you last tapped
- The "Permit join via" field now suggests router/coordinator names from Zigbee2MQTT's own device list as you type, instead of needing them typed out exactly from memory
- Fixed the "Permit Join" toggle never actually reading Zigbee2MQTT's response correctly, so it could refuse to show as on - the response's "time" value is nested under a "data" key, not at the top level of the payload
- Added a "Permit Join" banner right on the Home screen (one per broker) so devices can be let onto the network without leaving the dashboard to find it on a broker's edit screen - shows a live countdown of how long joining stays open, and now reflects Zigbee2MQTT's own ongoing bridge state instead of just a one-off request/response, so it also notices joining being opened or closed from elsewhere (e.g. Zigbee2MQTT's own web frontend)
- Fixed the "Permit Join" countdown showing an absurd multi-decade time remaining - Zigbee2MQTT's "permit_join_end" is already an exact timestamp (milliseconds), not seconds, so it was being scaled up 1000x more than it should have been
- The last router (or "Coordinator") used with "Permit Join" is now remembered as soon as the toggle is actually used, not just when "Done" is tapped afterwards - it's pre-filled next time, and the Home screen's own Permit Join banner picks up the same value too
- Tapping the Home screen's Permit Join banner (not its switch) now jumps straight to that broker's own Permit Join section on the edit screen, instead of needing to scroll down to find it again
- Fixed a real cold-start stall (confirmed via logcat on an underpowered TV: over 12 seconds to first frame, then several more seconds of skipped frames) - loading your saved configuration from disk was blocking the main thread before the app could even start drawing. It's now loaded in the background like everything else that does real file I/O, with no change in what appears on screen once it's ready
- Fixed the TV side navigation rail being nearly unreadable in dark mode (dark text/icons on a dark background) - it was using the wrong colors entirely, unrelated to the rest of the app's actual light/dark theme, so it now correctly tracks whatever theme the app is actually in
- Fixed TV: pressing Back to escape a focused text field could silently do nothing at all once the on-screen keyboard had actually been used, leaving focus stuck there with no way out - Compose's own focus-clearing call has a documented exception for exactly that case that wasn't being accounted for
- Added Up/Down as a second, more direct way to escape a focused text field on TV, alongside Back - a single-line field has no real use for vertical cursor movement anyway, so this always moves focus away in one press, and unlike Back it isn't affected by the on-screen keyboard consuming the first press of it to dismiss itself
- Fixed the "Permit join via" suggestion dropdown being completely unreachable on TV - nothing inside it ever received focus on its own when it opened, so Down now jumps straight into the list instead of just escaping the field like it does everywhere else
- Fixed every plain on/off Switch row in the app (Permit Join, Authentication, Clean Session, Auto Connect, Background Work, Blink Warnings, Smoke Alerts, and more) being unreachable with a TV remote - pressing Up/Down between the rows above and below one skipped straight past it every time, since it was a small target off on the right edge rather than spanning the row like everything else. The whole row is now one target, tap/click included, matching how this kind of row is meant to work
- Fixed tiles not shrinking as much as they should at a small "Tile & Cluster Width" setting - each text line's own reserved vertical space wasn't shrinking along with its font size, leaving noticeable empty space above/below the value and label on a small tile even though the text itself had gotten much smaller
- Home screen's "no groups yet, tap + to add one" message now only shows once your saved configuration has actually finished loading - on a slow cold start it could otherwise flash up for a moment even when you already have groups configured, telling you to do something you've already done. It now says the app's still loading instead, for that brief window
- Fixed TV: on a cold start, the remote's D-pad could actually be focused on the Terminal rail icon even though Home correctly showed as the selected one - two separate startup checks were racing each other, and the second one bumped focus one step past where the first one had already (correctly) put it
- A cluster's own title and "last seen" text now shrink along with its tiles at a small "Tile & Cluster Width" setting too, instead of staying full (phone-sized) above a much smaller row of tiles - floored at a smaller shrink than the tiles themselves get, since it's a heading read once for the whole row rather than per-tile decoration
- Fixed a genuinely serious TV bug: pressing Down while the Settings rail icon had focus didn't just fail to move focus, it lost focus completely, with no button combination able to get it back short of force-closing and reopening the app. Down is now safely ignored at that bottom edge of the rail instead
- On TV, every text field across the app no longer pops up the on-screen keyboard just from D-pad focus passing through it on the way to somewhere else - it now only appears when you actually press OK on that field, using Compose's own built-in support for exactly this (so OK still explicitly summons it, same as ever). Phone/tablet behavior (keyboard appears on tap) is unchanged
