- Fixed dashboard group order sometimes coming back different after wiping app data and re-adding devices: groups now reliably rebuild in the order their "/app" configs declare, regardless of what order the broker happens to redeliver them in.
- Fixed a deleted panel (e.g. one of several tiles duplicated from an auto-published device) sometimes reappearing after editing another panel in the same cluster: deleting a panel now also removes it from that device's own "/app" config, instead of leaving it to get silently rebuilt.
